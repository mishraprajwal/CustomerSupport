# Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the product is unused.
After 30 days, refunds are issued as store credit only. Digital subscriptions are refundable within 14 days
of the first charge. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Refund Exceptions

Custom-made items and gift cards are non-refundable. Orders flagged for fraud review cannot be refunded until
the review (usually 48 hours) completes.

# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.
Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Tracking numbers are emailed once the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Lost Packages

If a package shows no tracking movement for 7 days, open a carrier investigation and offer the customer a
replacement or refund. Escalate to Tier 2 if the order value exceeds $500.

# Troubleshooting Errors

## ERR-4012 Payment Declined

ERR-4012 means the card issuer declined the payment. Ask the customer to verify the billing address and try a
different card. Do not retry the charge more than twice to avoid fraud locks.

## ERR-5003 Login Loop

ERR-5003 occurs when stale cookies conflict with the new SSO session. Ask the customer to clear cookies or use a
private window, then sign in again. If it persists, reset the password from the admin console.
